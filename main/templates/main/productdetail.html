Product Information

The Padonmar Cheik is a meticulously handwoven fabric that embodies the essence of Myanmar's centuries-old Acheik tradition. Each piece is crafted with precision on a traditional loom by skilled artisans in Mandalay, requiring weeks of dedicated weaving to achieve its complex patterns. The lotus-inspired motifs represent purity, harmony, and resilience, while the wave-like designs capture the flowing beauty of Myanmar's rivers. Woven from a blend of high-quality silk and cotton, the Padonmar Cheik offers a luxurious sheen, breathability, and long-lasting durability. This textile is not only a fashionable statement but also a cultural treasure passed down through generations. Wearing the Padonmar Cheik means carrying a piece of Myanmar's heritage that combines artistry, symbolism, and elegance.

Garment Care

To maintain the vibrant colors and delicate texture of your Padonmar Cheik, careful maintenance is essential. For everyday use, avoid frequent washing as this reduces the life of natural fibers. When washing becomes necessary, always use a mild, silk-safe detergent and cold water. Hand washing is strongly recommended, but if machine washing is unavoidable, use a gentle cycle and place the fabric inside a protective mesh laundry bag. Never wring the garment as this may distort its weave; instead, gently press out excess water with a clean towel. Air drying in the shade is ideal to preserve the natural sheen and prevent color fading. Proper care ensures your Padonmar Cheik retains its beauty and structure for years to come.

Special Care Instructions

Because the Padonmar Cheik is made from fine silk and cotton threads, it requires extra attention during storage and handling. For long-term care, dry cleaning by a professional is highly recommended to protect the intricate weaving and ensure colors remain vibrant. Avoid using bleach, stain removers, or harsh chemicals, as these can permanently damage fibers. When ironing, always use the lowest silk setting, place a thin cloth over the fabric, and iron while slightly damp to prevent heat marks. Store your garment in a breathable fabric bag rather than plastic to avoid moisture buildup and mildew. Keeping your Padonmar Cheik in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight will help preserve its delicate texture and cultural value.
