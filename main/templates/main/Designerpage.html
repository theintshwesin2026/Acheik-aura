Myo Min Soe

Modern Myanmar fashion designer

Myo Min Soe is a highly acclaimed Myanmar fashion designer, best known for his mastery in haute couture and evening wear. With a career spanning decades, he has built a strong reputation for creating luxurious gowns that combine timeless elegance with modern sophistication. His designs often feature detailed handwork, intricate embellishments, and graceful silhouettes that highlight both beauty and craftsmanship.

He has dressed many celebrities, pageant contestants, and high-profile figures, especially for prestigious events like Miss Universe Myanmar. His creations have not only elevated Myanmar's fashion scene but also gained international recognition, showcasing the richness of Myanmar's culture blended with global trends. Through his work, he continues to redefine glamour and bring a unique identity to Myanmar fashion on the global stage.

Beyond designing, Myo Min Soe plays an influential role in inspiring young designers and supporting the growth of Myanmar's fashion industry. His dedication, creativity, and innovative spirit have made him a role model in the field. Today, he stands as a symbol of excellence in couture, carrying Myanmar's fashion heritage forward with elegance and pride.

May Myat Warso

Luxury bridal & haute couture designer

May Myat War Soe is a celebrated Myanmar fashion designer admired for blending traditional aesthetics with modern elegance. With years of dedication to preserving Acheik weaving and Myanmar's textile heritage, she has become a leading voice in promoting sustainable fashion and innovative design. Her commitment reflects not only in her creations but also in her efforts to keep cultural artistry alive.

Specializing in luxury haute couture, bridal wear, and contemporary adaptations of traditional fabrics, May's collections are praised for their intricate craftsmanship, timeless silhouettes, and meaningful storytelling through fabric. Her ability to merge cultural identity with modern trends makes her designs stand out both locally and internationally.

Beyond her fashion career, she actively supports cultural preservation projects, mentors young designers, and empowers local weaving communities. With vision and passion, May Myat War Soe continues to showcase Myanmar's heritage on the global fashion stage, inspiring a new generation while honoring tradition.

Mogok Pauk Pauk

Ceremonial & traditional dress designer

Mogok Pauk Pauk is a legendary Myanmar fashion designer celebrated for her mastery of ceremonial and traditional attire. She is best known for reviving the elegance of Myanmar's royal court fashion, making her creations highly sought after for cultural events, weddings, and prestigious ceremonies. Her designs carry a deep sense of heritage, connecting modern audiences with the grandeur of Myanmar's past.

She is widely respected for her meticulous handwork, luxurious choice of fabrics, and unique interpretations of ceremonial costumes that remain authentic while embracing modern aesthetics. Each piece she creates reflects a balance of tradition and artistry, showcasing the richness of Myanmar's culture through fine details and graceful silhouettes.

Beyond her achievements as a designer, Mogok Pauk Pauk has played a vital role in preserving Myanmar's ceremonial traditions. Through fashion shows and international exhibitions, she has brought Myanmar's cultural identity to global audiences. Her dedication and influence continue to inspire younger generations of designers while reinforcing the pride and beauty of Myanmar's fashion heritage.

FAQ

How to get designer service?
+
To use our designer service, first add your desired Acheik fabric and longyi to the cart. Then go to the "Designer" page, choose your preferred design and designer, and fill out the customization form.
How long is the waiting time?
+
It usually takes around 7–10 business days for your custom Acheik to be completed, depending on the complexity of the design and designer availability.
How to choose the design?
+
On the designer page, you'll see a gallery of designs under each designer. You can preview the samples and select the one that matches your style and the fabric you chose.
What to fill in the customization form?
+
In the form, please fill in:
  • Your body size chart (e.g., bust, waist, hip, etc.)
  • The fabric and textile names (choose from your cart)
  • Any special notes or instructions for the designer
This helps the designer tailor the dress perfectly for you.
Can I use fabric already in my cart?
+
Yes! The form allows you to link the fabric and textiles you added to your cart, so your selected designer will know exactly what to work with.
